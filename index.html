About the studio

Everything here is made by one person, in one room, usually over a few evenings.

We work in cotton and milk-cotton yarn, stuff firmly so pieces keep their shape, and stitch faces and details by hand rather than gluing on parts. Every piece is checked before it's wrapped, and leaves with a small card telling you how to keep it clean.

Because batches are small, stock numbers on each piece are real. If something you want has sold out, the custom form is the fastest way to get it remade.

  • MaterialsCotton and milk-cotton yarn, recycled poly fill
  • Sizes8 cm charms up to 25 cm plushies
  • CareSpot clean, air dry, keep out of strong sun
  • DeliveryAcross India, free above ₹1,500
